    Each comment is in Ze-Notes' parts. A summary of what the passage says is always there; a paraphrase and a translation of the quote can be added below it.

    Papers

    Each ticked paper is read on its own, one after another, and its proposals are kept. Nothing is saved to Zotero here: press Review on a paper to look over its proposals and save them. Your ticks are remembered for this collection. The first time, papers read before are not ticked; reading one again adds a new pass beside the earlier ones.
